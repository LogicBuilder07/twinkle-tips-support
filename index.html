Grown-up settings

Settings, buying, and sharing are behind a grown-up check: press and hold the button, then answer the math question. Kids who can't answer go back to the game.

Unlock Everything

The free version includes Lila, every skin tone, the Natural shape, the Pastels colors, and the full salon on one finger. The one-time Unlock Everything purchase adds every friend and their moms, being the customer with your own picture and name, all colors and shapes, stickers and glitter, pedicures, and all five fingers.

Restore a purchase

  1. Open the grown-up Settings and pass the grown-up check.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases.

Use the same Apple Account you bought with. If Family Sharing is on for the purchase, family members can restore it too.

"Be the customer" picture and name

The picture and the first name your child types stay on this device only. They are never uploaded or shared. To remove them, open grown-up Settings and tap Delete my picture and name. You can turn camera access off any time in the iOS Settings app.

Saved pictures

Finished nails are saved inside the app on this device. A grown-up can send or print a saved look after the grown-up check. Delete saved looks in grown-up Settings.

Something not working?

  • Make sure the app is up to date in the App Store.
  • No sound? Check the sound button in the game, the Ring/Silent switch, and the volume.
  • Still stuck? Email us with your device (for example, iPad Air) and what happened.

Privacy Policy

Twinkle Tips has no ads, no tracking, and collects no data.
