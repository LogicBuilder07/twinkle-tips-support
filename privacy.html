Twinkle Tips Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Short version: Twinkle Tips does not collect, store on a server, or share any information about you or your child. There are no ads, no analytics, and no accounts.

What the app keeps, and where

Everything stays on your device:

  • Saved looks (pictures of finished nails) are stored in the app on this device. You can delete them in the app's grown-up Settings.
  • "Be the customer" picture. If a grown-up has unlocked it and your child takes a picture, the app cuts the person out of the photo on the device and saves only that picture on the device. It is not uploaded, is not added to iCloud backups, and is never sent anywhere. Your child can also type a first name so the salon can greet them; the name is kept only in the app's settings on the device and is never sent anywhere. You can delete the picture and the name in Settings.
  • Settings such as music, sounds, voice, and quick mode, plus which stickers your child has collected, are stored on the device.

Camera

The camera is used only when your child chooses to take a picture to become a salon customer. The picture is processed on the device. The app asks iOS for permission first, and you can turn camera access off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

The optional "Unlock Everything" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Buying is only possible after a grown-up check.

Sharing and printing

A grown-up can send or print a saved look after passing a grown-up check. The picture goes only where the grown-up chooses, using the system share sheet or AirPrint. The app does not send it anywhere on its own.

Children's privacy

Twinkle Tips is made for children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children. There is no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or social features, and no links out of the app except the system share sheet behind the grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
